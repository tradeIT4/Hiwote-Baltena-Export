Our Story

A Legacy of Quality, Since 2000 E.C.

Our company was established 18 years ago by my mother, Birke G/kidan. For more than a decade, we have been serving the local community, building a strong and reliable reputation through sheer dedication and hard work.

We specialize in preparing all the vital food ingredients that are used in traditional Habesha cuisine. After successfully supplying the local market, we are now proud to step into the international trade arena, bringing the vibrant flavors of Ethiopia to the world.

Our Mission

To provide premium Ethiopian spices and food products that meet international quality standards while promoting Ethiopia's rich culinary heritage.

Our Vision

To become one of the leading exporters of Ethiopian spices and food products, recognized globally for quality, reliability, and customer satisfaction.
